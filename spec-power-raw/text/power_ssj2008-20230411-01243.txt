94 > /sys/devices/system/cpu/cpufreq/conservative/up_threshold

* echo 93 > /sys/devices/system/cpu/cpufreq/conservative/down_threshold

* echo 16000000 > /proc/sys/kernel/sched_latency_ns                            
* echo 9000000 > /proc/sys/kernel/sched_min_granularity_ns                     
* echo 240000 > /proc/sys/vm/nr_hugepages                                      
* cpupower frequency-set -u 2300MHz

0x2b0001b0 for the Intel Xeon Platinum 8490H processor.
